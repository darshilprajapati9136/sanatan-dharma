# Product Discovery & V1 Strategy

> Original full brief (862 lines). The implementation summary lives in
> `Product Discovery & V1 Strategy.md` in this folder; the build record is in
> `QA & Release Checklist — Sanatan Dharma Platform.md`.

**Project:** Sanatan Dharma Digital Platform  
**Status:** V1 Direction Locked  
**Purpose:** College project now, potentially expandable into a real consumer product later.

---

## 1. Why This Document Exists

The project originally started as an educational website for learning about Sanatan Dharma.

Initial planned areas included:

- Learn
- Scriptures
- Concepts
- Search
- AI-powered questions
- User accounts
- English and Hindi content

During development, one major weakness became clear:

**The product explained Sanatan Dharma, but it did not yet give users a strong reason to return regularly.**

Secondary market research was therefore conducted using:

- established Panchang platforms
- Hindu devotional applications
- newer Sanatan learning applications
- app-store feedback
- community discussions
- publicly available competitor information

Because of the academic deadline, primary interviews and surveys were not conducted.

Therefore, this document represents an **evidence-informed product decision**, not complete customer validation.

---

# 2. Original Problem Statement

The original assumption was:

> People interested in Sanatan Dharma need a reliable and modern website where they can learn about scriptures, concepts, traditions and practices.

This problem is real, but it is too broad.

Many websites, videos, apps and AI systems already provide religious information.

A static educational library alone is unlikely to create strong repeat usage.

---

# 3. Revised Problem

Users often face several connected problems:

### Problem 1 — Daily relevance

People may want to know:

- today's Tithi
- today's Nakshatra
- current Paksha
- Rahu Kaal
- important Muhurat
- today's Vrat
- today's festival
- upcoming observances

This information changes continuously and creates recurring use.

---

### Problem 2 — Information without understanding

Traditional Panchang products can provide large amounts of information, but beginners may not understand:

- what the information means
- which information matters
- why a particular festival is celebrated
- why certain timings are considered important
- why different calendars sometimes disagree

---

### Problem 3 — Fragmentation

Users often need different platforms for:

- Panchang
- festival information
- scriptures
- Mantras
- Puja information
- learning
- explanations

The experience is fragmented.

---

### Problem 4 — Trust

Religious information online can contain:

- unsupported claims
- missing sources
- conflicting interpretations
- inaccurate AI-generated answers
- unexplained differences between traditions

For this product, **trust must be treated as a core feature rather than a secondary consideration.**

---

# 4. Product Opportunity

The strongest opportunity identified is not simply:

> Build a better-looking Panchang.

Nor is it:

> Build another Sanatan Dharma encyclopedia.

The opportunity is to connect:

**Daily life + understanding + practice + learning**

into one coherent experience.

---

# 5. Product Vision

## Long-Term Vision

> Build a trusted digital companion that helps people understand and practise Sanatan Dharma in everyday life.

The platform should answer four questions:

### What matters today?

### Why does it matter?

### What can I do?

### Where can I learn more?

---

# 6. Core Product Model

The product will be organised around four connected pillars.

## 1. TODAY

Daily Hindu calendar and religious context.

Examples:

- Tithi
- Nakshatra
- Paksha
- Sunrise
- Sunset
- Rahu Kaal
- auspicious periods
- festivals
- Vrats
- upcoming observances

This becomes the main recurring-use layer.

---

## 2. UNDERSTAND

Explain the meaning behind the information.

Examples:

- What is Ekadashi?
- Why is Maha Shivaratri observed?
- What is Rahu Kaal?
- Why does Tithi differ from a normal date?
- Why do two Panchangs sometimes show different festival dates?

---

## 3. PRACTISE

Help users act on the information.

Examples:

- simple Puja guide
- Mantras
- Aarti
- Vrat information
- preparation checklist
- traditional practices
- reminders in future versions

Content should distinguish between:

- widely followed practices
- regional customs
- tradition-specific practices

---

## 4. LEARN

Structured long-term education.

Examples:

- Foundations of Sanatan Dharma
- Dharma
- Karma
- Bhakti
- Yoga
- Bhagavad Gita
- Upanishads
- Ramayana
- Mahabharata
- philosophical schools
- important concepts

Learning should be guided rather than functioning only as a document library.

---

# 7. Product Positioning

The platform should not position itself as:

> Another Panchang website.

It should also not position itself only as:

> A website about Hinduism.

Preferred positioning:

> **A trusted daily companion for understanding and practising Sanatan Dharma.**

---

# 8. Primary Target Audience for V1

V1 should primarily focus on:

### Young and beginner users

Especially people who:

- are interested in Sanatan Dharma
- know basic traditions but not their explanations
- want simple explanations
- use mobile devices heavily
- may find traditional Panchang interfaces overwhelming

Secondary audiences include:

- families
- existing Panchang users
- students
- Indian diaspora
- users wanting reliable religious references

Expert Panchang users are important, but V1 should not be designed exclusively around them.

---

# 9. Main Differentiation

The product should compete through the following combination.

## A. Progressive simplicity

Beginners should initially see:

- what matters today
- important timing
- festival/Vrat
- short explanation

Advanced information should remain accessible through:

**View Detailed Panchang**

This prevents information overload.

---

## B. What + Why + How

Religious events should connect three layers:

### WHAT

What is happening?

### WHY

Why is it significant?

### HOW

How is it traditionally observed?

---

## C. Source transparency

Important religious claims should eventually show:

- source
- text/reference
- translation where applicable
- explanation
- tradition or interpretation where relevant

AI should not invent religious claims.

---

## D. Tradition awareness

The system should avoid presenting every practice as universally identical.

Where relevant, information may indicate:

- regional differences
- calendar traditions
- Sampradaya differences
- alternative interpretations

---

## E. Connected experiences

Panchang, festivals, learning and scriptures should not behave like completely separate websites.

Example:

**Janmashtami**

→ Today's event

→ Why Janmashtami?

→ Puja information

→ Krishna Mantra

→ Bhagavata Purana reference

→ Krishna learning lesson

→ Related Bhagavad Gita content

# 12. V1 Feature Scope

The following are **IN SCOPE**.

### Core

- professional responsive homepage
- Today dashboard
- Panchang page
- festival/Vrat listing
- festival detail pages
- Learn section
- Scriptures section
- Search
- English
- Hindi
- authentication
- user profile
- source attribution
- methodology/about pages

### AI

AI may support:

- natural-language search
- explanation
- source retrieval
- navigation

AI must not be treated as an authoritative generator of Panchang calculations.

---

# 13. Explicitly Out of Scope for V1

Do NOT add the following before the college deadline:

- astrology consultations
- paid priests
- live Puja booking
- ecommerce
- donation marketplace
- Kundali generation
- horoscope marketplace
- social networking
- community feeds
- direct messaging
- temple booking
- large creator platform
- livestreaming
- complex gamification
- native mobile app
- dozens of personalised dashboards

These belong to future versions.

---

# 14. Panchang Accuracy Rule

Panchang information must never be generated through an LLM.

Required architecture:

**Astronomical/calendar computation or verified structured data**

→ validation rules

→ structured database/API representation

→ presentation layer

AI may explain the result.

AI must not calculate or guess the result.

---

# 15. Source Architecture

Religious content should ideally follow:

**Claim**

↓

**Primary or authoritative source**

↓

**Translation**

↓

**Explanation**

↓

**Tradition / interpretation**

↓

**Related content**

This will later support the RAG system.

---

# 16. AI Behaviour

AI answers should avoid unsupported statements such as:

> “Sanatan Dharma says…”

when multiple traditions or interpretations exist.

Where appropriate, answers should distinguish:

- scriptural statement
- traditional interpretation
- regional custom
- philosophical interpretation
- modern explanation

AI responses should cite available source material.

---

# 17. Design Principles

The visual experience should feel:

- calm
- respectful
- contemporary
- premium
- human-designed
- culturally grounded

Avoid:

- excessive gradients
- generic AI-looking cards
- enormous amounts of text
- decorative Sanskrit everywhere
- excessive saffron
- overwhelming animations
- repetitive layouts
- unnecessary glassmorphism
- excessive religious imagery

Use motion primarily for:

- navigation
- state changes
- information hierarchy
- interaction feedback

not decoration.

---

# 18. Mobile-First Requirement

The main V1 flows must work exceptionally well on mobile.

Priority:

1. Today
2. Panchang
3. Festival
4. Search
5. Learn
6. Ask
7. Profile

Desktop should provide additional space rather than a completely different experience.

---

# 19. Future Differentiator to Research

A promising future capability is:

## “Why does my Panchang differ?”

Potential experience:

User selects:

- location
- regional tradition
- calendar preference

The system explains why another source may show a different:

- festival date
- Vrat
- Tithi
- Muhurat

This feature should only launch after calendar rules and scholarly sources have been properly validated.

---

# 20. Success Criteria for the College V1

The project does not need millions of users to succeed.

For the submission, success means demonstrating:

### Product thinking

Clear user problem.

### UX quality

Professional responsive experience.

### Engineering

Real architecture rather than static mockups.

### Trust

Sources and methodology.

### Utility

A meaningful daily-use experience.

### Content

Useful educational material.

### Integration

Panchang → festival → explanation → scripture → learning.

### Polish

Few broken or unfinished experiences.

---

# 21. Product Development Rule

From this point until submission:

> **Depth before breadth.**

Do not start another major feature until the core flows work properly.

Priority order:

Today

→ Panchang

→ Festival experience

→ Learn

→ Scriptures

→ Search

→ AI

→ personalisation

---

# 22. Validation Status

Validated using secondary research:

- strong demand exists for Panchang/calendar utilities
- recurring daily information creates repeat-use potential
- substantial competition already exists
- modern educational Hindu applications are emerging
- source trust and regional differences are meaningful issues
- overwhelming information can create UX problems

Not yet validated through primary research:

- exact preferred homepage layout
- willingness to pay
- strongest target demographic
- importance ranking of every Panchang component
- demand for tradition-comparison functionality
- long-term retention behaviour

These should be validated after the academic deadline if development continues commercially.

---

# 23. Final V1 Product Statement

> **A modern Sanatan Dharma platform centred around daily relevance. It helps users discover what matters today, understand why it matters, practise it with confidence, and continue learning through trustworthy, source-connected content.**

---

# 24. Decision

The previous product direction is not discarded.

Existing:

- Learn
- Scriptures
- Search
- RAG
- authentication
- localisation
- profile
- design system

remain valuable.

However, **Today and Panchang now become the primary product entry point**, while educational content becomes connected to the user's daily context.

This document supersedes conflicting strategic assumptions in earlier planning documents.
