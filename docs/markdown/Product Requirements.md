# Product Requirements Document

## Current V1 direction — 19 September 2026

V1 is a trusted daily companion for understanding and practising Sanatan Dharma. Priorities: Today, Panchang, Festivals/Vrat, guided Learn, Scriptures, Search, safe Ask, and preserved authentication. Four pillars: TODAY → UNDERSTAND → PRACTISE → LEARN. The historical feature lists below are a long-term backlog, not the submission acceptance scope.

# 1. Product Overview

**Working Product Name:** To be decided

**Product Type:** Responsive educational and spiritual web platform

**Primary Market:** India

**Initial Languages:** English and Hindi

**Future Platform Expansion:** Android and iOS applications

**Product Vision**

Create one of the most trusted, accessible, and beginner-friendly digital platforms for understanding Sanatan Dharma.

The platform should help users learn about Sanatan Dharma through authentic sources, structured explanations, scriptures, philosophy, traditions, practices, festivals, mantras, and AI-assisted learning.

The long-term objective is to make reliable knowledge about Sanatan Dharma easy to access while respecting its diversity of traditions and interpretations.

# 2. Problem Statement

Information about Sanatan Dharma is currently spread across books, websites, videos, social media, different traditions, and multiple languages.

A beginner may face several problems:

- They may not know where to start.
- Different websites may provide contradictory information.
- Religious information may be presented without sources.
- Complex Sanskrit or philosophical concepts can be difficult to understand.
- Users may not know whether something comes directly from scripture or from a later interpretation.
- Valuable knowledge is often scattered across many different websites and applications.
- Some content online may oversimplify or misrepresent traditions.
- Beginners may find traditional texts difficult to understand without explanations.

The proposed platform will organize reliable information into one structured and understandable learning environment.

---

# 3. Proposed Solution

Create a bilingual digital platform where users can:

- Learn Sanatan Dharma from beginner to advanced levels.
- Read and understand major scriptures.
- Explore major concepts and philosophies.
- Learn about deities and traditions.
- Read mantras and stotras.
- Understand major festivals.
- Search across religious content.
- Save important verses or pages.
- Ask questions through an AI learning assistant.
- View the sources used for important religious information.

The platform should combine traditional knowledge with a modern user experience.

# 5. Product Principles

The platform should follow several core principles.

**Accuracy Before Quantity**

It is better to publish fewer high-quality, properly sourced pages than thousands of unreliable pages.

**Source Transparency**

Users should be able to understand where important religious information comes from.

**Respect for Different Traditions**

Sanatan Dharma contains many philosophical schools and traditions.

The platform should avoid presenting one interpretation as the only possible interpretation when respected traditions disagree.

**Scripture vs Interpretation**

The system should clearly distinguish between:

1. Original scripture
1. Translation
1. Traditional commentary
1. Modern explanation
1. AI-generated explanation

**Beginner Friendly**

Complicated ideas should be explained in understandable language without unnecessarily removing their philosophical depth.

**Bilingual First**

Hindi and English should both receive proper support.

Hindi should not simply feel like an automatically translated version of the English website.

**Technology Supports Learning**

AI should help users understand Sanatan Dharma.

---

It should not present itself as a guru, acharya, priest, or unquestionable spiritual authority.

# 15. AI Safety and Accuracy Requirements

The AI should:

- Prefer verified platform sources.
- Provide citations wherever possible.
- Clearly distinguish scripture from commentary.
- Avoid inventing verses.
- Avoid inventing Sanskrit quotations.
- Avoid presenting uncertain claims as facts.
- Mention when traditions interpret a subject differently.

---

- Avoid declaring one sect or tradition superior.
- Avoid claiming divine authority.
- Allow users to view the original source.

For sensitive theological questions, answers should acknowledge respected differences between traditions.

# 30. Major Risks

**Religious Accuracy**

Incorrect information could damage trust.

**Mitigation:** sourcing and verification system.

**AI Hallucination**

AI could generate incorrect verses or interpretations.

**Mitigation:** source-grounded retrieval and citations.

---

**Sectarian Bias**

The platform could unintentionally favour one tradition.

**Mitigation:** clearly identify interpretations and traditions.

**Massive Scope**

Sanatan Dharma contains enormous amounts of literature.

**Mitigation:** build gradually instead of trying to cover everything immediately.

**Copyright**

Modern translations or commentaries may be copyrighted.

**Mitigation:** verify licensing and use public-domain or properly licensed content.

**Translation Quality**

Poor Hindi or English translations could change meanings.

**Mitigation:** human review and trusted translations.
